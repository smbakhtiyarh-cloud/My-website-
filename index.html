THE SMALL PRINT

Good to know.

Questions before you crunch? Start here.

How do I place my order?+

Add your snacks to the bag, enter your name and delivery details, then choose “Send order on WhatsApp.” WhatsApp opens with your order prepared. Review it and press Send to actually place the order.

Which areas do you deliver to?+

Delivery coverage and fees depend on your business settings. Please confirm your service areas and delivery charges with us on WhatsApp before placing an order.

How can I pay?+

Payment arrangements should be confirmed with the shop when your order is acknowledged. Add your actual accepted payment methods to the store information before launch.

Can I order for a gathering or event?+

Absolutely — use the Bundles category to start, then mention any event quantities or special requests in the order notes.
